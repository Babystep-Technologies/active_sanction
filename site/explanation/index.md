# Explanation

Why the library works the way it does. Discursive rather than instructive: an
explanation never tells you to do anything.

Planned: how matching works, why the default threshold is 75, and where the
open gem ends and the commercial service begins.

The short version of the last one, which is also the version in the README:
**the gem is complete on its own.** Every supported list is fetchable,
parseable and screenable locally, forever, with no account and no key. A
commercial hosted service exists and what it sells is operations rather than
capability.
